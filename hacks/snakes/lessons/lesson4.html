Cybersecurity involves protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks. Encryption is one powerful technique used to scramble data so that only authorized parties can read it.

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage or gain unauthorized access to a system. Understanding malware types helps you recognize and defend against threats.

⚠️ Common Types:

  • Viruses: Attach to files and spread when shared
  • Worms: Self-replicate across networks
  • Ransomware: Encrypts files and demands payment

💡 Always keep your antivirus software updated and avoid downloading files from untrusted sources!

Encryption

Encryption converts plaintext into ciphertext using an algorithm and a key. Only someone with the corresponding key can decrypt the message, ensuring privacy and security.

🔐 How it works: When you visit a website with HTTPS, encryption scrambles the data traveling between your browser and the server. Even if someone intercepts the data, they can't read it without the decryption key!

Real-world example: When you send a message like "Hello" through encryption, it might become something like "X7k2pQ9" during transmission. Only the recipient with the key can convert it back to "Hello".

💡 Always look for the padlock icon in your browser's address bar—it means your connection is encrypted!
