Lesson 5: Computing Impacts & Data Analysis

Computers have transformed society in many ways. Big data and analytics allow us to discover patterns and make predictions, but they also raise important questions about privacy, bias and ethical use of information.

Big Data

Big data refers to datasets that are too large or complex to be processed by traditional tools. Specialised technologies like Hadoop and cloud computing enable analysis of these massive datasets.

Privacy & Ethics

Collecting and analysing personal data can lead to discoveries that benefit society, but mishandling data can harm individuals. Ethical computing emphasises transparency, fairness and respect for user privacy.
